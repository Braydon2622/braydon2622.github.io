Sales Cheat Code

In daily use · rebuilt in n8n

An AI sales assistant that tells loan officers exactly what to do next: the text to send back, a softer backup, the next step and when to follow up. I built it, trained my whole team on it, then rebuilt it as four connected automations.

Result9 of 9 reps use it daily. Each saves 30 minutes a day, 4.5 hours a day across the team, and follow ups run an estimated 20% faster.

The full story

Problem

Reps were guessing when to follow up and what to say back to client texts, so leads went cold from hesitation, not lack of effort, and every rep handled the same situation differently.

What I built

First a chat assistant the team uses on every call and text. Then four connected n8n workflows that run on their own: morning follow up emails, automatic call analysis, the chat coach and the scoreboard.

How I got it adopted

Trained the team with real scenarios from their own week, kept the output short enough to read on a phone, and cut anything reps skipped.

Safeguards

Texts avoid words texting platforms flag and never quote rates or approvals. Sensitive numbers never reach a recap or the sheet. A licensed person sends every message.

Sales Cheat Code Live

Built and tested · pilot pending

An AI call coach for loan officers. It listens to a call, spots coaching moments like objections, missed next steps and compliance slips, and shows the rep a short suggestion card. A review app turns recorded calls into coaching reports.

ResultOn 25 held out synthetic test calls: 79% precision, 92% recall, 18 of 18 compliance slips caught and 0 non compliant cards out of about 400. The first suggestion appears in 1.6 seconds.

Live call→AI spots the moment→Compliance rules check→Suggestion card for the rep
Code, tests and eval results on GitHub
How it works

Problem

Coaching usually happens days after a call. Reps need the right move while the client is still on the line, without any risk of a compliance slip.

What I built

A live coach plus a rules check that blocks any suggestion breaking lending compliance before the rep sees it, and a review app for recorded calls. Backed by 136 automated tests.

Next step

Tested on synthetic calls so far. Next is leadership and compliance approval for a pilot on my team's recorded calls.

Stitch Bench

Live AI · working

A multi agent AI design team that turns a photo or idea into embroidery machine files.

ResultPhoto in, validated VP3 and DST machine files out, plus a thread list, sew out guide and cost per piece.

Photo or idea→AI designer drafts→2 AI reviewers critique→Designer revises→Machine checks
How it works

Problem

Turning a photo into a design an embroidery machine can sew takes design skill plus technical stitch knowledge most people don't have.

What I built

A designer agent drafts original art, a stitch inspector and an art director critique it, the designer revises, then the tool converts it to stitches and runs automatic checks.

Why it matters

The review loop catches problems before anything gets stitched, the same draft, review and revise pattern teams use with AI at work.

LO Command Station

Live AI · prototype

A gamified coaching center where an AI lead plans a loan officer's day and an AI crew drafts the scripts.

ResultA working prototype built privacy first: no borrower data is ever entered.

How it works

Problem

Loan officers need daily coaching, ready to use scripts and a plan for the day, but borrower data can't go into outside AI tools.

What I built

An AI lead (ATLAS) reads weekly goals and the funnel, picks a focus and sends missions to an AI crew that drafts templates and coaching. The loan officer approves everything.

Privacy by design

Templates use placeholders and the funnel is tracked as counts only, so no client details ever reach the AI.

If you hire me

My first 90 days rolling out AI

The same playbook I used with my own team, written for yours. Tools change; the order of operations doesn't.

Days 1 to 30Listen and find the frictionShadow the people doing the work, map the workflows, time the manual steps and pick the 2 or 3 tasks where people guess, repeat themselves or wait. Learn the data and privacy rules first and record a baseline.
Days 31 to 60Build small and pilotBuild simple tools with approved AI for those tasks. Pilot with a few early champions, train with their real situations, collect feedback every week and cut anything people skip.
Days 61 to 90Roll out and measureTrain the whole team in short hands on sessions, run office hours and publish a shared prompt library. Champions coach their peers. Report time saved, adoption and quality, then pick the next problem.

My rules

Privacy first. A person approves anything that reaches a customer. A simple tool that gets used beats a perfect one that doesn't.

What I measure

Hours saved per person, weekly active users, quality checks on AI output and the business number the team already cares about.

Proof it works

On my own team: found reps stuck writing follow ups, built Sales Cheat Code, trained all 9. All 9 use it daily.
