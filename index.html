Work · Sales Cheat Code

Two problems my sales team had, and the AI I built to fix them

Calls went sideways with no one there to coach, and reps lost hours a day deciding what to text. I built a coach for each, then trained the team until they used them.

1
Problem one · phone calls

Sales Call Cheat Code: training wheels for every call

The problem: new reps learn calls by making mistakes on real clients, and a manager can only listen to a few calls a week. Nobody tells the rep what they could have said until it's too late.

Live call · 02:14Example · role play call
Voice 1 → Loan officer Voice 2 → Client
  • ⚡
    Coaches you during the call. When the client raises an objection or asks something tricky, a card shows what to say and what to ask next, about 1.6 seconds after they stop talking.
  • 🗣️
    Knows who's talking. It tells the two voices apart by how they sound, so the coaching is about what the loan officer said, not the client.
  • 📝
    Reviews every call after. What went well, what went wrong, what you could have said instead, the turning point, and a follow up text ready to send.
  • 📊
    Shows where calls go wrong across the team, so coaching targets the real patterns instead of guesses.

Approach

How I'd roll out AI on your team

The same order of operations I used with my own team. Tools change; this doesn't.

Days 1 to 30Listen and find the frictionShadow the people doing the work, time the manual steps, and pick the 2 or 3 tasks where people guess, repeat themselves or wait. Learn the data and privacy rules first and record a baseline.
Days 31 to 60Build small and pilotBuild simple tools with approved AI for those tasks. Pilot with a few early champions, train with their real situations, and cut anything people skip.
Days 61 to 90Roll out and measureShort hands on sessions for everyone, office hours and a shared prompt library. Report time saved, adoption and quality, then pick the next problem.

My rules

Privacy first. A person approves anything that reaches a customer. A simple tool that gets used beats a perfect one that doesn't.

What I measure

Hours saved per person, weekly active users, quality checks on AI output, and the business number the team already cares about.

Proof it works

On my own team I found reps stuck writing follow ups, built the coach and trained all 9. All 9 use it daily.
