Gremlets Support

Gremlets answers Claude Code from your iPhone and Apple Watch while you're away from your computer. Something not working? Email braydon@braydonhartai.com and say which device and what you see. I usually reply within a day or two.

Getting set up

  1. Install the Gremlets app on your Mac. On a PC, also install Gremlets for Windows.
  2. In the Gremlets menu on the Mac, choose Add iPhone Widget and scan the QR code with your iPhone's camera.
  3. Choose Set Up the iPhone Remote for Claude Code in the same menu. New Claude Code sessions pick it up.
  4. Allow notifications when the iPhone app asks.

Common questions

When do prompts go to my phone?

After you've been away from the computer for 5 minutes. Touch the computer again and anything still open goes back to the terminal.

The widget says the Mac is asleep

The Mac hasn't checked in for over an hour: it's asleep, offline, or the Gremlets app isn't running. Open Gremlets on the Mac and the widget catches up on its next refresh. Opening the iPhone app refreshes it right away.

My Allow or Reply didn't reach Claude

Make sure the iPhone is signed in to iCloud and online, then open the Gremlets app once. If you touched the computer before answering, the question had already gone back to the terminal.

What does Discard do?

It puts Claude's changes away with git stash, so nothing is lost. Run git stash pop to get them back.

Managing Gremlets Plus

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Restore a purchase from the Plus card in the app.
