Gremlets Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Gremlets doesn't collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. This page covers the Gremlets iPhone and Apple Watch app, the Gremlets Mac app and Gremlets for Windows.

What stays on your devices

Your code, your Claude Code sessions, your files and your diffs stay on your own computers. The apps keep their settings, the pairing between your devices, and the last widget picture on the device itself.

What travels between your devices

To bring Claude's questions to your phone and your answers back, your computer and your phone pass small notes to each other: what's waiting for you, a session's status and timer, your reply, and (if you ask for a review) the changes Claude made. Before a note leaves a device it is sealed with AES-GCM encryption using a key that exists only on your paired devices. It's shared once, when you pair by scanning a QR code.

The sealed notes travel through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and, on older setups, the public relay ntfy.sh. Neither can read them, and neither can we. Alerts reach your phone through Apple's push service, sent directly from your own computer. The push tokens that make this work are inside the sealed notes.

Weather

If you set a city in the Mac app, it asks a public weather service for that city's forecast to draw the weather in the widgets. Only the city goes out, never your location from your device.

Purchases

Gremlets Plus subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; the app only checks whether your subscription is active.

Children

Gremlets is a tool for developers and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no information from anyone.

Deleting everything

Unpair in the app (or delete it) and the pairing key is gone. The sealed notes on iCloud can't be opened without it.

Changes and contact
