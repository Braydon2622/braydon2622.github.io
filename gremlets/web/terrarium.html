🌿 Your Terrarium

The Gremlets are starting from nothing: an empty glass room. While you work they'll plant moss, build ledges, grow a tree, hang little homes and invite critters, a bit more every day, faster whenever Claude is busy on your Mac. Every finished task plants a flag and gets a cheer.

Tap a Gremlet to poke it, hold to pet, drag to toss. Tap the moss to plant a flower.

  1. Add to Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there: full screen.
  2. Keep it on its charger with Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never.
  3. To keep it on just this: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, then triple-click the top button.
