Working

Stitch Bench

A multi agent AI design team that turns a photo or idea into embroidery machine files. A designer drafts, two AI reviewers critique, the designer revises, and automated checks validate the stitches.

Photo in. Validated VP3 and DST machine files out, with a thread list, sew out guide and cost per piece. Shown here: a design it turned into a file, then sewn for real.

Live on Amazon

Azoki Market

An AI crew runs my woven blanket store built on public domain art like Hokusai's Great Wave. It builds listings in Printify, publishes to Amazon and reports sales.

Launched October 2026 with 12 products. Anything that spends money waits for my approval.

Its own storefront is live at azokimarket.com and opening soon, with prints, tapestries and glow boxes from the worlds of Azoki Studios.

I design physical products, too

Before I built anything with AI, I taught myself product design. Every piece here started as my own sketch, became a 3D model I built in Blender, and ended up as a real object you can hold. No AI touched any of it.

Manufactured and sold

A vinyl figure, from sketch to collectors' shelves

No background in toys or factories. I drew the character, modeled it in Blender, designed the window box, then worked with an overseas factory through samples and changes. I sold the final run to collectors through my own site and Instagram ads, and buyers posted it when it arrived.

"The hardest part was figuring out how to make something from scratch, and the problem solving it took to learn every new skill along the way."
